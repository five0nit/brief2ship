## Important conservative license boundary

Exact supported identifiers and complete MIT grant text with fixed titles may be
normalized. A complete MIT body with free-form copyright/surrounding prose gets
`license_body_match=MIT`, `normalized_license=null`, `license_review_required=true`.
No regex can reliably distinguish arbitrary holder names from arbitrary conditions.
Ordinary copyright-bearing MIT files therefore require review too. Diagnostic body
recognition must never be treated as license authority.
